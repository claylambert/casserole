The struct `HeatInner` is the lock and the wait signal shared with the heatbar worker.
The field `slot` is a mutex around a [`HeatSlot`](struct.HeatSlot.md). A poisoned mutex still yields the inner value. The field `cv` is the condition variable the worker and the window thread signal.
