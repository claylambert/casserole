struct FaultSeat

The struct `FaultSeat` is the status text and the optional notice for one wire fault.
A caller applies `status` to the status line and opens `slide` when it is present.

The field `status` is the status-line word.
The field `slide` is an [`ErrorNotice`](struct.ErrorNotice.md) when a notice should open.
`slide` is `None` when the fault stays on the status line.
