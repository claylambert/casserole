enum PaneNode

The `PaneNode` enum is one node in the pane tree.
[`PaneNode::Leaf`](#member-leaf) holds one pane id. [`PaneNode::Split`](#member-split) holds a [`SplitWay`](enum.SplitWay.md), a ratio, and two child nodes.

`way` is horizontal or vertical. `ratio` is the first child's share of the span after the gap. `left` is the first child. `right` is the second child.
