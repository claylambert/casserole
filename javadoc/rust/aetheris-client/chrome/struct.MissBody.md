struct MissBody

The struct `MissBody` is a parsed fault body from `parse_miss`.
A caller reads the reason and the message to decide the status line and the notice.

The field `structured` is true when the text starts with `reason:`.
The field `reason` is the unescaped text of a `reason:` line.
An unstructured body leaves `reason` empty.
The field `message` is the unescaped text of a `message:` line.
An unstructured body stores the whole text in `message`.
