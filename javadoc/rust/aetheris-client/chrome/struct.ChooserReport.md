struct ChooserReport

The struct `ChooserReport` is the bounds and the path printed by a file chooser.
`parse_chooser_report` fills it. A caller saves the bounds and reads the path.

The field `bounds` is x, y, width, and height when a bounds line has a positive size.
`bounds` is `None` when no usable bounds line was printed.
The field `file` is the chosen path.
`file` is `None` when no path was printed.
