# Crate aetheris-term

[Rust tree](../index.md) · **aetheris-term**

**Source** `aetheris/rust/aetheris-term/src/lib.rs`

The crate exports a VT100 [`Grid`](grid/struct.Grid.md), a key encoder, and the [`Connector`](connector/trait.Connector.md) trait.

The contract is the terminal library serial, sections 6 through 13, and the VT100 slice. `feed` borrows the grid for the call. A [`Cell`](grid/struct.Cell.md) owns its character, its width, and its color. No reference into the read buffer survives `feed`. The core does not start a runtime. The core does not call [`Connector`](connector/trait.Connector.md).
